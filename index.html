Summary

My work leans toward the data-and-infrastructure side of AI — streaming pipelines, vector search, multi-database architecture — but I build full products end to end when a project calls for it, including the deployment.

Most of my coursework projects don't stop at a working demo on my laptop; they end up live, with a URL, an APK, or a running deployment. I'm currently open to freelance work and collaborations where I can build real systems at scale.

How I approach a project
01

Understand the actual constraint

Whether it's "use only foundational libraries" or "must support real-time data," I design around the real limitation rather than a simplified version of it.

02

Build the full pipeline

Data layer, logic layer, interface — connected end to end, so the system works as a whole rather than as disconnected pieces.

03

Ship it, not just submit it

Where the project allows it, I deploy — a live URL, a published app, or a working demo — since that's the difference between a grade and a portfolio piece.
